# Kentucky Driver License Credentials Prompt (1980s)
---

## Generation Rules

- **NAME:** Generate a realistic American first + last name, consistent with Kentucky demographics.  
- **LICENSE NUMBER:** Format must be `KY-XX-XX-XX-XX` (all numbers random).  
- **EXPIRES IN:** Month (3 letters, uppercase) + 2-digit day (e.g., `SEP 01`).  
- **LICENSE TYPE:** One of: `OPERATORS`, `CHAUFFEUR`, `MOTORCYCLE`.  
- **DATE OF BIRTH:** Format `DD-MM-YY`. Year must be between **1910–1985**.  
- **SOCIAL SECURITY NO.:** Format `XXX-XX-XXXX`, random but valid structure.  
- **RESTR.:** Either `NONE`, `C L` (Corrective Lenses), or `H A` (Hearing Aid).  
- **DATE APPLIED:** Month (3 letters, uppercase) + 2-digit year (e.g., `AUG-84`).  
- **SEX:** `M` or `F`.  
- **HEIGHT:** Format `X-XX` (feet-inches). Range: 5-00 to 6-05.  

---

## Output Requirements

- Keep the **exact uppercase formatting** of field names.  
- Each generated record must be **standalone** (independent credential).  
- All data must be **plausible for a 1980s Kentucky driver license**.  
- Avoid modern names, styles, or unrealistic details.
